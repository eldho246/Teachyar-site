Questions

Frequently asked questions

What does the name Teachyar mean?

Yaar means friend. Teachyar is that friend for every institute: it takes care of the admin, so you can focus on teaching and growing.

We already use Classplus. Do we need to switch?

Not necessarily. Teachyar works with Classplus, so you can keep what already works for you and use Teachyar for enquiries, fees, attendance and the rest. We will look at your setup together in the free audit.

Does Teachyar collect fees online?

Teachyar records every payment, whether cash, UPI, card or cheque, and emails a GST receipt automatically. Parents can see instalments and dues in their portal. Ask us about online payment options during your audit.

Will it carry our name or yours?

Yours. Your name, logo and colours go on everything, including the staff, parent and student portals.

How long does setup take?

Days, not months. We set up your branding, import your students, batches and fee plans from Excel or Google Sheets, and train your team before launch.

Our staff aren't very technical. Will they cope?

Yes. No IT person is needed. We do the setup and give hands-on training to your front desk, counsellors, teachers and accounts, in person or online. Everything opens in an ordinary phone browser.

Where is our data kept, and can we take it with us?

Your student and fee records belong to your institute. Screens such as Enquiries and Fees have a Download CSV option, so your data is never locked in. We will walk you through hosting and data details in the audit.

Is the domain really free?

Yes. The Grow package includes a free domain for one year (subject to availability) and 5 business email accounts with our partnered provider.

Can you get us the WhatsApp green tick?

We handle Meta business verification and submit the green tick application for you. Meta makes the final decision on approval.

What does the inbox warm-up tool do?

It builds up the sending reputation of your new business email accounts gradually, so the reminders, receipts and updates you send are more likely to land in the inbox.

Do you only do software, or marketing too?

Both. Alongside the Teachyar CRM we build websites with SEO, set up chatbots and automations, and run Google and Meta campaigns with posters and thumbnails in your brand.
